Pull up a chair

No more hopping between five launchers

Every game you own, gathered into one quiet place, so the one you want is always right there.

Every store, one shelf

Sign in once per store and your whole collection shows up. A game you own twice is one tile, and Cornerplay remembers which launcher you prefer for it.

Proton, sorted

Windows games run through Proton without any fiddling. Pick from every Proton build on your PC, download GE-Proton or Proton-CachyOS, and set any of it per game.

TV mode for the couch

Laid out like SteamOS Game Mode: big covers, trailers playing behind the text, and a controller-first layout with a proper on-screen keyboard.

Sales you'll actually see

Steam and GOG wishlist games on sale sit right above your library, with a heads-up when a new one drops, plus this week's free games on Epic.

Playtime, backlog, stats

Hours played on every store, Continue playing, achievements, star ratings, notes, collections, and a "What should I play?" button for undecided evenings.

Save backups and Health check

Saves are backed up before every launch. When a game closes straight away, Cornerplay reads the log and tells you why, with a button that fixes it.

Covers that keep up

Art from the stores, Steam and SteamGridDB, refreshed when it changes. Animated covers in TV mode, and a look that can follow your wallpaper's colours.

Steam and Game Mode friendly

Add your Epic, GOG and Amazon games to Steam with full artwork, so they show up in Game Mode on a Steam Deck too.

Private by design

Sign-ins and keys stay readable only by you, backups leave secrets out, and every download is checked against its publisher's checksum.

TV mode

Made for the sofa and a controller

Press F11, or the Guide button on your pad. Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo and Steam Deck controllers all work, and the on-screen hints match your pad's own buttons.

  • GuideThe main menu slides in from the left
  • Guide + AQuick access: now playing, downloads, quick switches
  • StartThe selected game's options
  • BSteps back, never out of TV mode by surprise
Every game's page

Everything about a game, in one place

Play or install, DLC with prices, achievements, ProtonDB rating, time to beat, screenshots and trailers. Add your own status, stars and a note like "stuck on boss 3".

Install

Up and running in a minute

The installer checks what your PC still needs, asks once, and puts Cornerplay in your app menu. No root needed for Cornerplay itself.

curl -fLO https://github.com/raulgbh-ctrl/cornerplay/releases/latest/download/cornerplay.run && sh cornerplay.run

Or download cornerplay.run and run it. Already installed? Cornerplay offers new versions by itself.

git clone https://github.com/raulgbh-ctrl/cornerplay && cd cornerplay/packaging/aur && makepkg -si

Builds a proper pacman package, so updates come through your system like everything else. An AUR package is on the way.
